# Interview Stage

Interview preparation begins only after the technical topic is complete and the learner has supplied a target role. Do not silently assume a role.

## Evidence

Browse current Nowcoder pages before writing role-grounded questions. Record title, date when available, URL, original wording, reported follow-up, and what it tests. Distinguish exact quotations, reported follow-ups, and questions synthesized from multiple sources. If access is blocked, say so and do not invent source-backed questions.

## Practice

Present one to three compact questions tied to the completed topic. Ask one question at a time and wait for the learner's answer. After each answer, separate:

- factual correctness;
- missing reasoning;
- engineering evidence;
- expression quality.

Give a concise improved answer structure, not a memorized essay. Label reasonable inferred follow-ups as inferred rather than reported.
